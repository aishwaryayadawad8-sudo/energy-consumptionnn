How to Use

  1. Select Country: Choose the country you want to send the alert to
  2. Use Template (Optional): Click a template button to start with a pre-written message
  3. Write Subject: Enter a clear and concise email subject
  4. Write Message: Type your custom alert message
  5. Send: Click the "Send Custom Alert" button
Tip: Be clear and professional in your message. Include specific actions or recommendations for the country.
